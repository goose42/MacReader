I have been baking bread at home for about six years now, and the thing I keep learning is that patience matters far more than any special equipment or secret ingredient.

Start with a simple dough of flour, water, salt and a little yeast, mix it until no dry flour remains, and then simply leave it alone for a few hours on the counter.

When the dough has doubled, fold it gently a few times, shape it, and let it rest again before baking in a very hot oven until the crust is deep brown and sounds hollow.

Write down what you did each time, because small notes about timing and temperature are what turn a lucky loaf into a loaf you can repeat on any ordinary weekend.
